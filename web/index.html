Method and limitations

Supported histologic scores are 3+3 through 5+5. Patterns 1 and 2 are not assigned in contemporary diagnostic practice.

This risk classifier considers Grade Group, clinical T stage, PSA and available core data. Intermediate groups may be indeterminate without biopsy involvement data. The optional legacy very-low grouping also requires PSA density.

Privacy: Data are processed locally in this browser, with no external scripts, remote API calls, analytics, or server upload.
